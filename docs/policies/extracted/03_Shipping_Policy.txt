SHIPPING POLICY

Effective date: 28 September 2026
Website: https://glowwithsh.ai.studio/

We know customers want clear delivery information. This policy explains how GlowWithSH handles order processing and shipping.

1. WHERE WE SHIP

Our current serviceable locations and any shipping charges are shown at checkout. If a PIN code is not serviceable, the order may not be accepted or may need to be cancelled and refunded.

2. ORDER PROCESSING

Most orders are prepared for dispatch within 1–2 business days after successful payment and order confirmation.

Orders placed on weekends or public holidays may begin processing on the next business day.

3. DELIVERY TIME

Standard delivery is usually around 3–7 business days after dispatch, depending on the delivery location and courier service.

Remote or difficult-to-service locations may take longer.

Delivery timelines are estimates. Courier delays, weather, public holidays, transport disruptions, incorrect addresses, and other events outside our reasonable control can affect delivery time.

4. SHIPPING CHARGES

Any applicable shipping fee will be displayed before you complete your purchase. Promotional free-shipping offers, if available, may have separate conditions.

5. TRACKING

Where tracking is available, the tracking details may be shared through email, SMS, WhatsApp, or another contact method provided with the order.

Please use the same phone number and email address you normally monitor so delivery updates can reach you.

6. DELIVERY ADDRESS

Please check your name, phone number, house/building details, area, city, state, and PIN code carefully before placing the order.

If an incorrect or incomplete address causes a failed delivery, additional delivery charges or re-shipping may apply where permitted and reasonably required.

7. MISSED DELIVERY

If a courier attempts delivery and the package cannot be delivered because nobody is available, the address is incorrect, or the customer is unreachable, the courier may make another attempt or return the parcel according to its process.

Please respond to courier calls or messages where possible to avoid unnecessary delays.

8. PACKAGE ARRIVES DAMAGED

Please check the outer package when you receive it. If the parcel is visibly damaged, leaking, open, or tampered with, take clear photographs or a short video before opening where possible.

If the product inside is damaged, incorrect, or defective, contact us within 48 hours of delivery. Our Return and Refund Policy explains what information may be required.

9. LOST OR STUCK SHIPMENTS

If tracking does not move for an unusual period or the parcel appears lost, contact us with your order number. We will coordinate with the courier and update you once we receive information.

10. INTERNATIONAL ORDERS

Unless the website specifically says that international shipping is available, this policy should be treated as applying to domestic India deliveries only.
