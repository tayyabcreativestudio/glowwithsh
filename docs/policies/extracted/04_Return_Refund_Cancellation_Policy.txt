GLOWWITHSH
RETURN, REFUND & CANCELLATION POLICY

Effective date: 28 September 2026
Website: https://glowwithsh.ai.studio/

We want every order to reach you safely. Because skincare and beauty products are personal-use and hygiene-sensitive items, returns are limited. At the same time, we will review genuine issues such as wrong, damaged, or defective products.

1. ORDER CANCELLATION

You may request cancellation as soon as possible after placing your order.

Cancellation is generally possible only if the order has not yet been dispatched. Once an order has been shipped, cancellation may no longer be possible.

If a cancellation is approved after payment, the eligible refund will be sent through the original payment method or another reasonable method used by us.

2. CHANGE OF MIND

We normally do not accept returns or refunds simply because a customer has changed their mind, selected the wrong product, ordered the wrong variant, or no longer wants the item.

Please check the product details carefully before placing your order.

3. OPENED OR USED PRODUCTS

For hygiene and product-safety reasons, we generally do not accept returns of products that have been opened, used, tested beyond a reasonable inspection, or altered by the customer.

This does not remove any consumer rights that cannot legally be excluded, including rights relating to defective or incorrectly supplied goods.

4. WRONG, DAMAGED, OR DEFECTIVE PRODUCT

If you receive a product that is:

- Different from what you ordered
- Damaged during delivery
- Defective or otherwise not in acceptable condition when received

please contact us within 48 hours of delivery.

To help us resolve the issue quickly, please send:

- Your order number
- Your registered name and contact number
- Clear photographs of the outer package
- Clear photographs of the product and issue
- A short unboxing or product video, where available and requested

We may ask for additional information before approving a replacement or refund.

5. APPROVED RESOLUTION

After review, we may provide an appropriate resolution such as:

- Replacement of the affected product
- Refund of the affected product
- Another solution agreed with the customer, where appropriate

The final resolution depends on the nature of the issue, product availability, and applicable consumer law.

6. REFUNDS

Where a refund is approved, we will initiate it after the review is complete. The time taken for the amount to appear in your account can vary depending on the payment provider and bank.

Any refund will normally be made to the original payment method used for the order unless another lawful and practical method is agreed.

7. RETURN SHIPPING

Where we confirm that a return is required because the item was wrong, damaged, or defective, we will provide return instructions. Customers should not send a parcel back without contacting us first.

For customer-requested returns that are not covered by this policy, we are not required to accept the return.

8. PACKAGE DAMAGE OR TAMPERING

If your parcel arrives visibly damaged, leaking, open, or tampered with, please take photographs or video and contact us as soon as possible, ideally before using the product.

9. ALLERGIES OR SKIN REACTIONS

A personal skin reaction does not automatically mean that a product is defective. Before using a new skincare product, please read the ingredient list and directions and consider a patch test where appropriate.

If you experience a serious or persistent reaction, stop using the product and seek suitable professional medical advice. Contact GlowWithSH as well so we can record the issue and assist where reasonably possible.

10. NON-RETURNABLE SITUATIONS

A return or refund may not be accepted where the product has been used, damaged by the customer, altered, contaminated, or returned without prior approval, subject always to applicable law.
