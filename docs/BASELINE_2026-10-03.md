Source facts conflict: two tinted creams and two anti-acne gels have distinct IDs/slugs but similar names; seeded ingredient/benefit copy lacks supplier documentation. Existing product claims, sunscreen test claims, invented-looking stock imagery and founder biography require factual review. Preserve catalog IDs, names, prices and source content while documenting uncertainty.

Current production preview mode disables online payment creation; COD behavior still needs actual route regression testing. A successful upload alone does not demonstrate persistence across deployment.

Work order: access control/data integrity/payment checks → real HTTP regressions → initial HTML/indexation/canonical/schema → asset/mobile/accessibility improvements → keyword/competitor research and safe copy → full regression and required handoff reports.
