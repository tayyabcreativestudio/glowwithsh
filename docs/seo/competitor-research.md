## Opportunity and intent

Prioritize branded catalog terms and exact product names. Category pages own broad comparison intent, product pages own purchase intent, and journal pages own educational questions. Broad face-care and serum queries face established brands and marketplaces; that is a directional inference, not a numeric difficulty score. Whitening/acne/SPF labels are not evidence of efficacy. Do not target cure/treatment, guaranteed whitening, dermatologist-approved, certified or tested terms without documents.

No competitor wording, ratings, business facts, formulations or testimonials were copied. See keyword CSVs for the complete candidate map and explicit missing metrics.
