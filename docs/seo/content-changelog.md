- Homepage: brand/product-type title and description; crawlable category/product links; truthful Organization/WebSite markup. Existing CMS hero wording retained.
- /about: rewritten brand introduction, comparison guidance and three values; removed unsupported manufacturing/dermatology/cruelty-free storytelling. Founder name retained.
- /shop and each category: distinct H1/title/description; choosing-products section and support/shipping/returns links; filters remain interactive.

Existing merchant descriptions/ingredients/benefits were not independently substantiated or overwritten.

- Journal articles: unique metadata/Article markup and existing text in initial HTML. Articles were NOT rewritten into invented ingredient facts.
- Policies/founder/awards/custom pages: metadata/rendering added; no claim that their business facts or full policy wording have been rewritten or legally approved.
