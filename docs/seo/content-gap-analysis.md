# Content gaps and evidence gates

Implemented now: unique metadata for all public routes, product purchase guidance, category comparison guidance, About rewrite, product/category/support/policy links and crawlable initial HTML. The journal's existing articles received rendering and metadata; their medical/ingredient statements were not validated.

| Gap | Available evidence | Action / gate |
|---|---|---|
| Full ingredient lists, percentages, usage directions and contraindications | Catalog text only; no authenticated packaging/lab documentation | Owner supplies labels for each SKU. Avoid inventing formulation details or product-specific safety directions. |
| Acne/whitening/SPF efficacy and manufacturing claims | Existing names/copy; no tests/certificates | Obtain substantiation and appropriate professional review before expanding or promoting these claims. Product names remain merchant identifiers, not endorsement of efficacy. |
| Two Tinted day cream listings and two Anti acne gel listings | Same names/sizes, different slugs/SKUs/prices | Metadata uses SKU to distinguish. Owner must confirm variants vs duplicates. Do not merge URLs or prices speculatively. |
| Brand history/awards/testimonials | CMS and seed copy, not third-party proof | About rewritten without unsupported manufacturing/history claims. Founder/awards/CMS assertions require owner evidence. |
| Genuine product photographs | Several catalog images use Unsplash | Upload packaging/product photos through real media library. Never label generic stock imagery as a verified product photo. |
| Supplier, legal entity, returns address, registration and operational delivery facts | Incomplete/unverified | Owner/accountant confirms these. No legal-compliance guarantee. |
| Educational content | Existing three articles with formulation assertions | Future guides: comparing serums; day vs night cream; preparing a routine before an event. Publish only after factual review, without outcome timelines or medical promises. |

Each product has a distinct price/size/title and purchase-information presentation. This is not a claim that every existing formulation, benefit, suitability statement or journal claim is scientifically substantiated. More words are not a substitute for evidence.
